National Disaster Management Guidelines (NDMA) — Immediate Actions

  • 1

    Move away from steep slope edges, drainage channels, riverbanks, and old quarry locations immediately.

  • 2

    Do not sleep in ground-floor rooms directly facing unreinforced hillsides or retaining walls during intense rainfall night hours.

  • 3

    Prepare an Emergency Go-Bag containing Aadhaar/documents, necessary medication, torch, dry food, and power banks.

  • 4

    Avoid driving through hill ghat roads (e.g., NH-10 Sikkim, Charmadi Ghat KA, Rishikesh-Badrinath highway, Wayanad ghats) during red alert advisories.

Pan-India Helpline: Dial 112 (National Emergency Response System) or 1070/1077 (State Disaster Management Authority) for urgent rescue.

Geological Warning Signs — Evacuate Immediately If Observed

New tension cracks on slopes, paved roads, house foundations, or retaining structures.

Sudden muddy water discharge or rapid changes in natural spring flows on hill slopes.

Tilting of trees, utility poles, or fences along hill slumps.

Faint rumbling sounds that increase in volume or sound of snapping tree roots.
